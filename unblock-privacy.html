Privacy Policy

Unblock Blocks (com.mynblockbl0ck.unblock_block)
Developer: BogdanBobok · Effective date: 8 September 2026

This privacy policy explains what data the Unblock Blocks mobile application (the “Application”) collects, why it is collected, and who it is shared with. BogdanBobok (the “Service Provider”) operates the Application as an independent developer.

Data the Application collects

The Application does not ask you to register, does not create an account, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or location.

The following data is collected automatically by the advertising software built into the Application and is shared with Google:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier assigned to your device by Android. Used to select and display advertisements, to limit how often the same advertisement is shown, and to detect invalid traffic and fraud.
  • In-app interaction data — records of advertisements shown and tapped inside the Application. Used to display advertisements and measure their performance.

Collection of this data supports the free version of the Application. It stops entirely if you buy the “Remove ads forever” purchase described below: after that no advertisement is requested and the advertising software loads nothing.

Your game data

The Application stores your progress and preferences only on your device, in its own private storage. This includes the current level, the levels you have unlocked, the position of blocks in an unfinished level, the number of levels completed, and your settings for language, theme, sound, vibration, and hints.

None of this is uploaded, transmitted, or copied to any server, and neither the Service Provider nor any third party receives it. If Android Auto Backup is enabled on your device, your settings may be included in a backup to your own Google account; game progress is deliberately excluded from that backup.

Purchases

The Application offers a single one-time purchase, “Remove ads forever”, which permanently disables all advertising. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The Service Provider never sees or receives your card number, bank details, billing name, or address. The Application receives from Google only the fact that a valid purchase exists, and records that fact on your device so that advertising stays switched off, including when you are offline. Payment questions and refunds are handled by Google Play.

Network use

Apart from advertising and purchase verification, the Application connects to the internet for one purpose: the “Our apps” screen loads the names and icons of the developer’s other applications from their public Google Play pages. These requests contain no information about you or your use of the Application. The screen works without an internet connection, using data included in the Application itself.

Third-party services

The Application uses the following services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing

Advertising consent

Where required by law, including for users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the Application shows a consent form before advertising is loaded, using Google’s User Messaging Platform. If you decline personalised advertising, advertisements are still shown, but they are not selected based on your interests. You can change your choice at any time by resetting the advertising ID in your device settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Data security and retention

All data leaves your device over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The Service Provider operates no servers and stores no user data. The data described above is held by Google according to its own retention policies. Application settings and game progress are stored only on your device and are removed when the Application is uninstalled.

Deleting your data

Because the Service Provider does not store any of your data, there is nothing held by the developer that can be deleted on request. You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in your Android settings, and you can remove all locally stored data by uninstalling the Application. A completed “Remove ads forever” purchase remains attached to your Google account and is restored automatically if you reinstall the Application.

Children

The Application is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable age of digital consent). The Service Provider does not knowingly collect data from children.
