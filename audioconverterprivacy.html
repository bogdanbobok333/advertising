Privacy Policy

Audio Converter and Cutter (com.audioconverter999.audiocutter999)
Developer: BogdanBobok · Effective date: 7 September 2026

This privacy policy explains what data the Audio Converter and Cutter mobile application (the “Application”) collects, why it is collected, and who it is shared with. BogdanBobok (the “Service Provider”) operates the Application as an independent developer.

Data the Application collects

The Application does not ask you to register, does not create an account, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

The following data is collected automatically by the advertising software built into the Application and is shared with Google:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier assigned to your device by Android. Used to select and display advertisements, to limit how often the same advertisement is shown, and to detect invalid traffic and fraud.
  • In-app interaction data — records of advertisements shown and tapped inside the Application. Used to display advertisements and measure their performance.

Collection of this data is required for the free, advertising-supported version of the Application and cannot be switched off inside it.

Your audio files

The Application requests access to audio files so that you can choose which file to convert, trim, or merge. It reads only the files you select yourself through the system file picker.

All processing — conversion between formats, trimming, merging, volume normalisation, and fade effects — is performed entirely on your device. Your audio files are never uploaded, transmitted, or copied to any server, and neither the Service Provider nor any third party receives them or their contents.

Converted files are written to the output folder on your device, either the default music folder or a folder you choose yourself. They remain under your control, and the Application does not track what you do with them afterwards.

Network use

Apart from advertising, the Application connects to the internet for one purpose: the “Our apps” screen loads the names and icons of the developer’s other applications from their public Google Play pages. These requests contain no information about you, your files, or your use of the Application. The screen works without an internet connection, using data included in the Application itself.

Third-party services

The Application uses the following services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

Advertising consent

Where required by law, including for users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the Application shows a consent form before advertising is loaded, using Google’s User Messaging Platform. If you decline personalised advertising, advertisements are still shown, but they are not selected based on your interests. You can change your choice at any time by resetting the advertising ID in your device settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Data security and retention

All data leaves your device over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The Service Provider operates no servers and stores no user data. The data described above is held by Google according to its own retention policies. Application settings, such as the chosen theme, language, and output folder, are stored only on your device and are removed when the Application is uninstalled.

Deleting your data

Because the Service Provider does not store any of your data, there is nothing held by the developer that can be deleted on request. You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in your Android settings, and you can remove all locally stored data by uninstalling the Application. Audio files you have created remain in your chosen folder and can be deleted like any other file.

Children

The Application is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable age of digital consent). The Service Provider does not knowingly collect data from children.
