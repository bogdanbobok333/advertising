Privacy Policy Guitar Guide Pro (com.yGuitarPtoGuidel.guitarguidepro555) Developer: BogdanBobok · Effective date: 7 September 2026 This privacy policy explains what data the Guitar Guide Pro mobile application (the “Application”) collects, why it is collected, and who it is shared with. BogdanBobok (the “Service Provider”) operates the Application as an independent developer. Data the Application collects The Application does not ask you to register, does not create an account, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or location. The following data is collected automatically by the advertising software built into the Application and is shared with Google: Advertising ID — a resettable identifier assigned to your device by Android. Used to select and display advertisements, to limit how often the same advertisement is shown, and to detect invalid traffic and fraud. In-app interaction data — records of advertisements shown and tapped inside the Application. Used to display advertisements and measure their performance. Collection of this data is required for the free, advertising-supported version of the Application and cannot be switched off inside it. Microphone The Application requests microphone access for the built-in guitar tuner. The audio signal is analysed on your device in real time to determine pitch. It is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere, and neither the Service Provider nor any third party receives it. Purchases The Application offers a one-time purchase that unlocks the full version and removes advertising. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play. The Service Provider does not see, receive, or store your payment card details or billing address. The Application only asks Google Play whether the purchase exists, in order to unlock the corresponding features on your device. Third-party services The Application uses the following services, which have their own privacy policies: Google Play Services Google AdMob Google Play Billing Advertising consent Where required by law, including for users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the Application shows a consent form before advertising is loaded, using Google’s User Messaging Platform. If you decline personalised advertising, advertisements are still shown, but they are not selected based on your interests. You can change your choice at any time by resetting the advertising ID in your device settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Data security and retention All data leaves your device over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The Service Provider operates no servers and stores no user data. The data described above is held by Google according to its own retention policies. Application settings, including the record of your purchase, are stored only on your device and are removed when the Application is uninstalled. Deleting your data Because the Service Provider does not store any of your data, there is nothing held by the developer that can be deleted on request. You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in your Android settings, and you can remove all locally stored data by uninstalling the Application. Children The Application is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable age of digital consent). The Service Provider does not knowingly collect data from children. Changes to this policy This policy may be updated when the Application changes.
